1. What LeafID is

LeafID is a free app that suggests which plant is in your photo, keeps the plants you save in your Herbarium, and shows short stories about each species.

2. Identifications are suggestions

Identifications are made automatically and can be wrong. Never use LeafID to decide whether a plant is edible, poisonous, medicinal, or safe for people or pets. Do not eat, touch, or use any plant based on what the app says. If someone may have eaten or touched a poisonous plant, contact a doctor, a vet, or your local poison centre (in Spain, the Instituto Nacional de Toxicología: 91 562 04 20).

3. Stories are written by AI

The stories on each card are written with artificial intelligence and can contain mistakes. Traditional uses are cultural history, not medical or food advice.
