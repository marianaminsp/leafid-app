Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

In short:

  • We use your photos to identify plants and, when you save them, to build your Herbarium.
  • No ads, no selling data, no tracking across other apps. Usage analytics only if you allow it.
  • You can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app at any time.

1. Who we are

Leaf ID is an iPhone app for identifying plants and keeping a collection of the ones you find, made by the Leaf ID team (“we”). The data controller is Mariana Minafro Spinelli, Spain. Contact for anything in this policy: app.leafid@gmail.com.

2. Account information

New accounts are created with Sign in with Apple or Google. We receive your email address and, if shared, your name. Apple’s “Hide My Email” works with Leaf ID. Older accounts may use an email and password.

3. Plant photos and location

  • Photos you take or choose are sent to our server to identify the plant. If you save the plant, the photo and a processed card image are stored with your account. Before upload, photos are re-encoded, which removes camera details and GPS tags from the file.
  • If you allow location access, Leaf ID saves where you found each plant (exact coordinates and the nearby place name) with that plant. Location is used only while the app is open. You can turn it off in iOS Settings; identification still works without it.

4. Your Herbarium and account activity

The plants you save (names, card text, dates, places) are stored with your account so they sync across devices. We also keep your daily identification count (to apply the free daily limit) and the version and date of the Terms of Use you accepted.

5. Usage analytics (only with your permission)

After you sign in, Leaf ID asks whether you want to share usage data. If you tap Allow, the app sends events such as “a scan was made” or “a card was flipped”, linked to your account’s random ID, never to your email, name or photos. If you don’t allow it, or until you answer, nothing is collected. You can change your choice at any time in Druid → Privacy → Share usage data. Turning it off removes the analytics ID from your phone.

6. Crash reports

If the app crashes or hits an error, it sends a technical report (what failed, app version, device model and iOS version) so we can fix it. Reports are not linked to your account and don’t include your IP address.

9. Photo visibility

Saved photos are stored at private, randomly generated web addresses so they load quickly in the app. They are not listed or searchable, but anyone who has the exact address of a photo could open it. When you share a plant, you choose what to send.

10. How long we keep data

  • Account, Herbarium, photos, locations and Terms acceptance: until you delete your account.
  • Usage analytics: up to 1 year, and deleted with your account.
  • Crash reports: 30 days.
  • Server logs: about 1 day.

To delete everything, open Druid and tap Delete account. This permanently removes your account, saved plants, photos, locations and the analytics linked to your account.

11. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, export or delete your data, to restrict or object to how we use it, and you can withdraw any consent at any time (without affecting what was done before). Email app.leafid@gmail.com; we reply within one month. You can also complain to your data protection authority; in Spain, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (www.aepd.es).

12. Minimum age

You must be at least 16 to use Leaf ID. We don’t knowingly collect data from younger children. If you believe a child has an account, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Changes

If we change this policy, we update the date above. For important changes we also tell you in the app.
